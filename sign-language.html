The project in simple points

What is SignBridge?

🧩

The problem

People may use different ways to communicate. This can make it hard to share basic needs in schools, clinics and public places.

🤝

Our solution

SignBridge brings picture choices, typed messages, spoken audio and sign-language learning resources together in one website.

🌍

Our goal

Support more inclusive everyday communication and help people learn how to communicate respectfully with one another.

How to use the official dictionary: Open the dictionary, search for the English word (for example, “Water” or “Thank You”), and watch the sign video. Follow the video rather than guessing a hand shape from a picture.
A responsible project

Why we use verified sign-language sources

Indian Sign Language is a complete language with its own grammar and regional variation. It is not simply English or Hindi represented by hand gestures. SignBridge therefore links to the official ISL Dictionary instead of inventing signs or claiming that its camera can translate ISL.

Official learning resource: Indian Sign Language Dictionary — Government of India ↗
The ISLRTC dictionary includes video signs and learning tutorials. See also the ISLRTC FAQ ↗ for information about the dictionary.

Future development: work with Deaf ISL users and qualified sign-language educators to add reviewed sign videos and test any sign-recognition feature before making translation claims.

A more inclusive way to connect

Communication should have no barriers.

SignBridge is a student-built accessibility prototype that helps people share everyday needs using pictures, written messages and spoken audio. It is designed to make communication easier when two people do not use the same communication method.

NIAT project overview

What problem does SignBridge solve?

In everyday places such as schools, clinics and public spaces, a person may find it difficult to communicate if they cannot speak, hear, read or type easily. SignBridge offers alternative ways to express common needs.

🎯

The problem

People can struggle to make themselves understood when communication methods differ.

💡

Our solution

A simple website with picture choices, typed messages, spoken output and optional voice input.

🧪

What this prototype proves

It demonstrates accessible communication tools and a limited live AI hand-pose demo. It is a prototype, not a finished medical or sign-language translation system.

How judges can test it: Open “Try the demo”, choose Picture board, tap “I need water”, then try “Speak message”. Next, open “Live AI demo” and allow camera access to test the hand-pose recogniser.

The challenge

Being understood should not depend on how we communicate.

Communication can be difficult when people use different methods—sign language, speech, text or pictures. SignBridge explores ways to make everyday interactions easier in places such as schools, clinics and public spaces.

🤟

Sign-language users

Offer sign-learning resources and a future pathway for recognising a limited, carefully tested set of signs.

🗣️

People with speech difficulties

Use large picture buttons and text-to-speech to express common needs without having to speak.

👵

Older adults & everyone

Use clear layouts, large controls, audio support and simple phrases that are easier to navigate.

Our approach

Built with people, not just for people.

Accessibility tools should be designed alongside the people who will use them.

Listen first

Ask users what situations are difficult and what support they prefer.

Build carefully

Use clear visuals, verified sign-language materials and privacy-conscious design.

Test & improve

Test with volunteers, fix confusing parts and explain the limits honestly.

Live AI demonstration

Try the camera hand-gesture demo

This uses an on-device computer-vision model to detect a small set of common hand poses in real time. It demonstrates the camera + AI interaction judges can test.

📷 Live camera

Press “Start camera demo” and allow camera access.

✨ AI detection result

Waiting for a hand gesture…

The model will show its detected hand-pose category and confidence score here.

Accuracy and inclusion note: This demo recognises a limited set of general hand-pose categories using MediaPipe's pre-trained gesture model. These categories are NOT a validated Indian Sign Language (ISL) translator, and the displayed labels must not be treated as ISL meanings. Real sign-language translation needs a properly sourced ISL dataset, motion and two-hand context, expert validation and testing with sign-language users. Camera frames are processed in your browser; the camera is used only after you grant permission. Stop the camera when finished.
